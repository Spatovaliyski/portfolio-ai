Bausy is the all-in-one pet care app built for dog owners, cat lovers and anyone caring for rabbits, ferrets or other companions. Replace scattered notes and paper folders with a single pet health tracker — vaccinations, medications, vet records, smart reminders, AI guidance and local service booking, all in one beautiful app. Whether you are a first-time pet parent or managing a busy household with multiple animals, Bausy helps you stay organized, proactive and confident about your pet's wellbeing. PET HEALTH PROFILE & VETERINARY RECORDS • Complete digital health card per pet — breed, age, photo and profile details • Vaccination tracker and vaccine reminder with next-dose dates and overdue alerts • Medication manager with dosage, schedule and linked intake reminders • Allergy tracker and chronic disease records for safer vet visits • Weight tracker with history, averages, trends and visual charts • Upload and store vet documents, lab results, prescriptions and medical files • EU pet passport, microchip number and identification details in one place • Customizable health card — show the sections that matter to you • Emergency QR code to share vital pet info with vets, sitters or finders instantly SMART REMINDERS & PUSH NOTIFICATIONS • Pet reminder app for vaccines, pills, flea treatments, grooming and vet appointments • One-time and repeating reminders — general tasks, appointments and medication doses • Push notifications delivered to your phone before important due dates • Home dashboard highlights overdue tasks, today's to-dos and what's coming next • Full pet activity log — see who changed what and when across all profiles AI PET ASSISTANT — SHARO • Personal AI assistant trained for pet care and aware of your pet's profile • Ask about symptoms, nutrition, training, behaviour, breed traits and daily routines • Urgent case guidance — calm, practical advice when something seems wrong • Curated prompts for emergencies, care tips, breed questions and obedience training • Daily pet care tips tailored to breed, age and season (Bausy+) WATCHDOG — PROACTIVE HEALTH ALERTS FOR YOUR PET • Smart alerts for upcoming vaccinations, ending medications and scheduled appointments • Weather-aware walk tips — heat, cold, rain, strong wind, hail and storms • Weight change notices, senior screening reminders and seasonal parasite alerts • Post-vaccination care guidance and allergy awareness notifications • Critical health alerts are always free — no subscription required FAMILY PET SHARING & COLLABORATION • Share pets with your partner, family members or pet sitter • Granular access — choose who can view or edit each animal's profile • Create a family group with invite codes or QR scan to join • Perfect when several people share daily care responsibilities MORE FEATURES PET OWNERS LOVE • Educational pet care articles and tips inside the app • Multi-language support — use Bausy in your preferred language • Light, dark and system theme for comfortable day and night use • Clean, modern design inspired by the best mobile apps • Free plan for core pet tracking; Bausy+ unlocks unlimited pets, documents, family sharing, smart insights, Watchdog extras and unlimited AI chat Download Bausy today — the pet health diary, vet record keeper, medication reminder, vaccination tracker and grooming booking app your companion deserves.
